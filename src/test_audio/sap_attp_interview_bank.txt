SAP ATTP Techno-Functional — High-Pressure Scenario Interview Bank
================================================================================
Role: SAP ATTP Techno-Functional Consultant (Foster City / remote-capable)
Scope: ATTP repository, MAH–CMO–3PL serialization, GS1 EPCIS, DSCSA / EU FMD,
       Dell Boomi integrations (AS2/SFTP/WS), GAMP 5 / 21 CFR Part 11 validation,
       RISE with SAP discovery impacts

Structure:
  10 themes × (1 main + 4 follow-ups) = 50 spoken prompts
  Follow-up 1–2: scenario ladders (increase stakes / multi-party conflict)
  Follow-up 3–4: deliberate single-word / Yes-No traps (create doubt, force commitment)
  After short answers: next prompt forces defense under intensity

Psych design (applied, not announced to candidate):
  • Commitment & consistency (Cialdini) — lock Yes/No then explode consequences
  • Cognitive load — multi-regulation + partner ops + middleware in one frame
  • Status challenge / dominance — rigid interviewer cadence, “prove me wrong”
  • Anchoring — assert a “correct” industry position, force pushback or collapse
  • Inconsistency trap — recycle earlier Yes/No against a new scenario
  • Expertise micro-trap — GLN, GTIN, SSCC, bizStep, disposition, EPCIS event fields
  • Moral/compliance pressure — patient safety, DSCSA, GAMP 5, Part 11 signatures
  • Time scarcity — go-live / freeze / war-room clocks
  • False dichotomy — ship vs compliance, fail-open vs fail-closed
  • Attribution pivot — “so the CMO is right and you are wrong?”

Tone for TTS: sharp, skeptical senior architect + QA/compliance lead hybrid.
Gap between spoken prompts: 12 seconds (answer window)

THEME 1 — AS-IS / TO-BE process ownership (MAH vs CMO vs 3PL)
================================================================================
Main (scenario):
You walk into a serialization program where Marketing Authorization Holder, two CMOs,
and one 3PL all claim “ATTP is already live.” Shipping works for full pallets, but
partial pallets, rework, and returns create orphan serials. How do you run discovery
to map AS-IS event flows and design a TO-BE process that ATTP can actually enforce?

Follow-up 1 (pressure):
Business wants TO-BE signed this Friday. CMO IT refuses workshops. 3PL only shares
a PDF from 2021. Compliance says DSCSA timeline is non-negotiable. What do you deliver
by Friday that is not fiction, and what do you explicitly refuse to sign?

Follow-up 2 (corner):
A VP says: “Standard ATTP covers this; stop over-engineering.” You believe partial-pallet
deaggregation at the 3PL is a real gap. How do you prove the gap with evidence, and how
do you present the decision options to leadership without looking political?

Follow-up 3 (single-word trap — doubt):
Yes or no only. Will you sign the TO-BE process as complete without a live CMO workshop?

Follow-up 4 (single-word trap — tighter):
One word. Approve, Conditional, or Reject — for the program go-live gate this sprint.

================================================================================
THEME 2 — Serial lifecycle: request, commission, pack, ship, receive
================================================================================
Main (scenario):
Define end-to-end how serial number requests, commissioning, packing, shipping, and
receiving events should flow between MAH, CMO, and 3PL on SAP ATTP, including who owns
which master data objects: GTIN, GLN, SSCC, and business partner.

Follow-up 1 (pressure):
CMO commissions under their GLN but ships under MAH branding. 3PL receives against a
different GLN than ATTP expects. Serials land as “unknown location.” Walk the failure
path event by event and show the corrected ownership model.

Follow-up 2 (corner):
Production already ran. Ten thousand serials are commissioned with wrong parent SSCC
aggregation. Shipping starts in six hours. What is your recovery sequence, and what
do you tell Quality if patient-impact risk is non-zero?

Follow-up 3 (yes/no trap):
Yes or no. Should shipping be blocked when parent-child aggregation is incomplete?

Follow-up 4 (single-word trap):
One word only. Who is accountable if those ten thousand serials ship broken — MAH,
CMO, 3PL, or Integration?

================================================================================
THEME 3 — Partner capability gaps vs standard ATTP
================================================================================
Main (scenario):
How do you systematically identify gaps between standard SAP ATTP capabilities and
partner operational realities: partial pallets, late aggregation, returns, rework,
and mixed-lot handling? Give me your gap matrix method.

Follow-up 1 (pressure):
3PL can only send daily flat files, not real-time EPCIS. Legal still wants DSCSA-grade
traceability. Design the interim control and the ATTP configuration boundaries you
will not cross.

Follow-up 2 (corner):
A partner “closes the gap” with a manual spreadsheet upload by warehouse clerks.
Validation and Part 11 teams call it a compliance bomb. Do you allow it for ninety days?
Defend your position with GAMP 5 language.

Follow-up 3 (yes/no trap):
Yes or no. Is a manual spreadsheet upload an acceptable EPCIS substitute in production?

Follow-up 4 (single-word trap):
One word. Risk rating for that interim — Low, Medium, High, or Critical.

================================================================================
THEME 4 — Global regulations: US DSCSA vs EU FMD
================================================================================
Main (scenario):
Your MAH ships US and EU packs through shared CMOs. Explain how ATTP design must differ
for US DSCSA versus EU FMD, and where a single global process will fail if forced.

Follow-up 1 (pressure):
A CMO wants one EPCIS map for both markets to save cost. You suspect that collapses
required fields and dispositions. How do you refute them with concrete message-level
examples, not slogans?

Follow-up 2 (corner):
EU affiliate says a US-only design is already live and “works.” A recall drill fails
to reconstruct pedigree across borders. Who failed — ATTP config, process, partner,
or requirements — and what is your 48-hour containment plan?

Follow-up 3 (yes/no trap):
Yes or no. Can one EPCIS Implementation Guideline fully cover DSCSA and FMD for this
network without market-specific variants?

Follow-up 4 (single-word trap):
One word. Primary root cause of the failed recall drill — Data, Design, Partner, or Process.

================================================================================
THEME 5 — ATTP master data: partners, locations, GTIN/SSCC
================================================================================
Main (scenario):
Walk me through how you configure ATTP repository master data for CMOs and 3PLs:
business partners, locations with GLNs, GTINs, and SSCC rules. What is mandatory before
the first commissioning event?

Follow-up 1 (pressure):
Two plants share a GLN by mistake. Commissioning succeeds; shipping events misroute.
How do you detect, correct historical events if possible, and prevent recurrence with
governance?

Follow-up 2 (corner):
Master data steward is on leave. Middleware is ready. Business wants to hardcode GLNs
in Boomi mappings “temporarily.” Your call as ATTP techno-functional lead?

Follow-up 3 (yes/no trap):
Yes or no. Will you allow GLNs hardcoded in Boomi for go-live?

Follow-up 4 (single-word trap):
One word. If you refuse and go-live slips, who do you escalate first — Business, IT,
Compliance, or Program?

THEME 6 — GS1 EPCIS design & Implementation Guidelines
================================================================================
Main (scenario):
Design the standard GS1 EPCIS message set for a CMO and a 3PL integrating to ATTP.
Which business steps and dispositions are mandatory for commissioning, packing,
shipping, and receiving in your IG?

Follow-up 1 (pressure):
Partner returns events with missing eventTime, wrong bizStep, and disposition “active”
on destroy scenarios. ATTP rejects half the feed. How do you triage: config vs mapping
vs partner process — in one working session?

Follow-up 2 (corner):
Partner claims your IG is non-standard and “not GS1.” You know your IG is a constrained
profile. How do you run the standards argument without losing the onboarding timeline?

Follow-up 3 (yes/no trap):
Yes or no. Is a missing eventTime always a hard reject?

Follow-up 4 (single-word trap):
Answer with one word only. For destroy events, the correct disposition should be… what?

THEME 7 — Rules, schemas, authentication, authorized exchanges
================================================================================
Main (scenario):
How do you configure ATTP rules, system schemas, and authentication so only authorized
partners can exchange serialization data with specific locations and products?

Follow-up 1 (pressure):
A rogue test certificate from a CMO still works in production and posts events against
a live GTIN. Security is furious. Reconstruct how this was possible and the permanent
control stack you implement.

Follow-up 2 (corner):
Business wants a “break-glass” shared technical user for all 3PLs during peak season.
Refuse or accept? Justify under Part 11 and operational reality.

Follow-up 3 (yes/no trap):
Yes or no. Shared technical users across partners — allowed in production?

Follow-up 4 (single-word trap):
One word. Maximum acceptable authentication model for partners — Certificate, Basic,
Token, or MutualTLS.

THEME 8 — Middleware (Dell Boomi) and connectivity patterns
================================================================================
Main (scenario):
Collaborate with middleware: how do you design secure partner connections for ATTP
traffic using AS2, SFTP, and web services, and what does the ATTP consultant own versus
the Boomi team?

Follow-up 1 (pressure):
AS2 works in lower environments; production AS2 fails intermittently with partner
timeouts. Serialization backlog grows. How do you run triage across network, Boomi,
ATTP, and partner — and what interim business process do you authorize?

Follow-up 2 (corner):
Boomi lead says “ATTP is rejecting good messages.” Your logs show schema violations.
How do you run a joint war room so the partner trusts the diagnosis?

Follow-up 3 (yes/no trap):
Yes or no. Should business shipping continue if ATTP event posting is delayed by more
than four hours?

Follow-up 4 (single-word trap):
One word. Preferred production connectivity for regulated event exchange with a mature
3PL — AS2, SFTP, or API.

THEME 9 — Partner onboarding & E2E testing
================================================================================
Main (scenario):
You are the primary technical contact guiding CMO and 3PL IT through testing and
onboarding. Describe the pipeline from IG sign-off to production cutover, including
E2E tests that simulate automated CMO production runs and 3PL fulfillment.

Follow-up 1 (pressure):
Partner passes unit tests but fails E2E when volumes hit ten thousand serials per hour.
Where do you look first — ATTP, Boomi, partner, network — and what performance evidence
do you demand before go-live?

Follow-up 2 (corner):
UAT sign-off is green, but a shadow production pilot shows 2% event loss. Program
wants to go live anyway. What is your recommendation, and how do you document residual risk?

Follow-up 3 (yes/no trap):
Yes or no. Do you approve go-live with two percent event loss?

Follow-up 4 (single-word trap):
One word. Your go-live vote — Go, NoGo, or Defer.

THEME 10 — Validation (GAMP 5 / 21 CFR Part 11) + RISE with SAP
================================================================================
Main (scenario):
Author validation deliverables for ATTP integration: Functional Spec, Configuration Spec,
and UAT scripts under GAMP 5 and 21 CFR Part 11. What must be testable, and what is
out of scope for electronic signature controls?

Follow-up 1 (pressure):
Auditor challenges that partner-facing configuration changes lack adequate audit trail
and dual control. How do you remediate without freezing all partner onboarding?

Follow-up 2 (corner — RISE):
Leadership asks you to lead discovery for RISE with SAP impact on ATTP, interfaces,
and validation. What risks do you put on the first slide for executives, and what
you will not estimate in week one?

Follow-up 3 (yes/no trap):
Yes or no. Does moving to RISE automatically re-validate all ATTP partner interfaces?

Follow-up 4 (single-word trap):
One word. Biggest RISE risk to ATTP — Connectivity, Validation, MasterData, or Timeline.
