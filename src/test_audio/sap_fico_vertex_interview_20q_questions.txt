SAP FICO + Vertex Tax — Scenario Interview Bank
Role focus: SAP FICO (Finance & Controlling) with Vertex tax determination / compliance
Structure: 20 original scenario questions × 2 laddered follow-ups each = 60 spoken prompts
Gap between every spoken question: 10 seconds
Difficulty ladder: Original (solid mid) → Follow-up 1 (harder scenario) → Follow-up 2 (hardest / multi-system)

================================================================================
Q1 — Month-end close & Vertex tax timing
================================================================================
Original:
A US company runs SAP S/4HANA Finance with Vertex O Series for sales/use tax.
On the last day of the month, Accounts Payable posts a large vendor invoice for a multi-state service.
The invoice posts in FI, but tax on the vendor side looks wrong versus Vertex reports.
Walk me through how you would investigate from an SAP FICO perspective.

Follow-up 1:
Finance needs to close GL by 6 PM. Vertex batch reconciliation is still running and two company codes
show tax differences under $500 and one shows $48,000. How do you decide what blocks close,
what you park, and what you escalate — and how do you document it for audit?

Follow-up 2:
Internal Audit later finds that the $48,000 gap was caused by a Vertex jurisdiction update
that was not reflected in SAP tax codes / condition records for one plant. Design the
end-to-end control (people, process, system) so this cannot recur next close without detection.

================================================================================
Q2 — Customer invoice tax determination (Vertex outbound)
================================================================================
Original:
Order-to-cash: a sales order ships from a warehouse in Texas to a customer in California
with mixed taxable and non-taxable line items. Explain how SAP SD/FI and Vertex interact
to determine tax on the billing document, and what FICO sees in the accounting document.

Follow-up 1:
The customer is partially exempt and provides a multi-jurisdiction exemption certificate.
Tax still calculated fully on two lines. How do you troubleshoot certificate setup,
customer master tax classification, material tax classification, and Vertex exemption mapping?

Follow-up 2:
Three months later, California audits the company and claims under-collection on those lines.
Vertex and SAP disagree on historical rates for that ship-from / ship-to combination.
How do you reconstruct the determination path for a single invoice and prepare a defense package?

================================================================================
Q3 — AP invoice with Vertex reverse charge / use tax
================================================================================
Original:
A plant in Illinois buys capital equipment from an out-of-state vendor. The vendor does not
charge sales tax. Explain how SAP FICO and Vertex would handle use tax accrual, and what
postings you expect in FI (tax accounts, GR/IR, asset or expense).

Follow-up 1:
The asset is capitalized under a different cost center and profit center than the original PO.
Use tax still posts to the wrong CO object. How do you correct open and closed postings
without breaking asset capitalization and depreciation already run?

Follow-up 2:
Tax wants monthly use-tax self-assessment files from Vertex, but Controllers want the GL
tax accounts to match Vertex to the cent before filing. Design a reconciliation process
and the SAP reports / tables / IDocs you would use each month.

================================================================================
Q4 — New company code go-live with Vertex
================================================================================
Original:
You are the FICO lead adding a new US company code that must go live with Vertex on day one.
What FICO configuration and master data must be ready before the first invoice posts?

Follow-up 1:
Cutover weekend: legacy open A/R and A/P are migrated without tax lines, but Vertex
expects historical transactions for returns. What is your cutover approach for tax continuity?

Follow-up 2:
Two weeks after go-live, intercompany billings between the new company code and an existing
one double-tax or zero-tax inconsistently. How do you diagnose intercompany tax determination
across company codes, plants, and Vertex taxpayer profiles?

================================================================================
Q5 — Cost center actuals vs tax expense allocation
================================================================================
Original:
Tax expense from Vertex-linked postings is landing on a default cost center, distorting
P&L by department. Business wants tax to follow the underlying expense cost center.
How would you approach this in SAP CO / FI with Vertex in the landscape?

Follow-up 1:
Some postings are pure tax adjustments from Vertex journals with no “underlying” expense.
Finance wants them allocated by a monthly statistical key figure. How do you design that
without double-counting tax already on invoices?

Follow-up 2:
After allocation, profit center reporting still does not match legal entity tax filings.
How do you explain the gap between management CO reporting and statutory Vertex filings
to a CFO in under five minutes?

================================================================================
Q6 — Vertex downtime during billing peak
================================================================================
Original:
Vertex is unreachable during a peak billing run. Describe what should happen in SAP
(fail-open vs fail-closed), and how FICO should respond operationally.

Follow-up 1:
Leadership forces billing to proceed with estimated tax to hit revenue recognition.
Next day Vertex is back. How do you re-rate, reverse, and repost tax cleanly in FI/CO?

Follow-up 2:
During re-rate, 2% of invoices cannot be reprocessed because of locked customers or
cleared payments. Design an exception factory and the accounting treatment for residual risk.

================================================================================
Q7 — Credit memos, returns, and tax
================================================================================
Original:
A customer returns goods two months after the original invoice. Original tax was calculated
via Vertex. Explain the SAP and Vertex flow for the credit memo and expected FI impact.

Follow-up 1:
The return is partial, price was adjusted after invoice, and the customer moved states.
Tax on the credit does not mirror the original invoice. How do you investigate and correct?

Follow-up 2:
Cash application already cleared the original invoice with a small underpayment written off.
How do you handle tax-only adjustments after clearing without reopening the full receivable mess?

================================================================================
Q8 — Tax code design in SAP vs Vertex authority
================================================================================
Original:
Historically the company used many SAP tax codes per state. They are moving determination
to Vertex and want fewer SAP tax codes. What is your recommended tax code / account key
design for FI, and why?

Follow-up 1:
Treasury and statutory reporting still need tax broken out by jurisdiction in the GL.
Vertex has the detail, SAP GL does not. How do you satisfy both Controllers and Tax?

Follow-up 2:
External auditors insist on a clear map from Vertex tax name / jurisdiction to SAP GL accounts
for every material tax type (sales, use, excise if any). Produce the control matrix you would own.

================================================================================
Q9 — Project system / WBS and Vertex
================================================================================
Original:
A customer project uses PS WBS elements billed on milestones. Some billings are taxable
services, some are exempt professional services. How should FICO and Vertex be set up
so project P&L and tax stay aligned?

Follow-up 1:
Milestone billing was reversed and rebilled after a contract amendment changed taxability.
CO actuals on the WBS no longer match billing documents. How do you clean this up?

Follow-up 2:
The project spans three states with remote workers. Vertex nexus rules change mid-project.
How do you handle mid-stream taxability change for open and future milestones?

Q10 — Vertex integration architecture (FICO ownership)
================================================================================
Original:
Explain the integration pattern you expect between SAP and Vertex (synchronous call,
batch, IDoc, API, middleware). As a FICO consultant, what do you own vs what Basis/Tax IT owns?

Follow-up 1:
A new e-commerce channel posts billing documents through a non-standard interface that
bypasses normal tax user exits. Invoices hit FI with zero tax. How do you detect and stop this?

Follow-up 2:
Enterprise architecture wants to replace the current Vertex connector with a custom BTP
integration in six months. What FICO regression test pack and cutover checklist do you demand?

Q11 — Foreign currency and tax
================================================================================
Original:
A US company code invoices a Canadian customer in CAD; company code currency is USD.
Tax must be handled carefully with Vertex. What are the FICO risks and how do you validate postings?

Follow-up 1:
Exchange rate differences on tax lines appear after payment. Business asks if tax base
should revalue. What is your recommendation and how do you configure/test it?

Follow-up 2:
Vertex returns tax in one currency while SAP document currency differs for a rush invoice
posted by a junior accountant. How do you detect, reverse, and prevent recurrence with controls?

Q12 — Manual FI journals vs Vertex truth
================================================================================
Original:
Tax team posts a manual JV in SAP to true-up sales tax payable to the Vertex return.
What controls should exist so manual tax journals do not silently break subledger integrity?

Follow-up 1:
The JV used a P&L tax expense account instead of a balance-sheet tax payable account.
Month-end ratios look wrong. How do you find the root cause and correct periods already closed?

Follow-up 2:
Management wants “no manual tax JVs” within 12 months. Design the automation and
exception-only workflow to get there, including roles in SAP and Vertex.

Q13 — Drop-ship and third-party tax scenarios
================================================================================
Original:
Goods ship from a third-party supplier directly to the end customer (drop-ship).
SAP has a PO and a customer billing. How should tax determination work with Vertex,
and what FICO postings do you validate?

Follow-up 1:
The supplier charges tax on their invoice incorrectly, and your customer billing tax
also looks wrong. How do you untangle AP tax, AR tax, and inventory/COGS impacts?

Follow-up 2:
Legal says risk of double taxation across two states on the same economic sale.
How do you model the transaction in SAP/Vertex and what evidence do you keep?

Q14 — Product taxability change (material master)
================================================================================
Original:
A material’s tax classification changes from taxable to exempt mid-year due to a
product redesign. What master data and open transaction impacts do you manage in SAP/Vertex?

Follow-up 1:
Open sales orders still explode old tax after the change. Some deliveries already done.
What is your wave plan for open orders, billing blocks, and communication to CS and Finance?

Follow-up 2:
Prior period returns of the “old” product version still need the old tax treatment.
How do you support dual taxability without corrupting current determination?

Q15 — Profit center / segment reporting with tax
================================================================================
Original:
The business runs document splitting and profit center accounting. Tax lines sometimes
do not split the way Controllers expect. How do you analyze a single billing document’s
tax lines through to FAGLFLEXT / ACDOCA?

Follow-up 1:
A shared service cost center bears all tax differences, breaking segment EBITDA.
Propose a sustainable design for tax difference handling under document splitting.

Follow-up 2:
After S/4 migration, historical tax balances in a reconciliation account do not
split by profit center. Auditors flag it. What is your remediation strategy?

Q16 — Vertex returns, filings, and SAP AP/AR subledgers
================================================================================
Original:
Walk me through a monthly sales tax return cycle: what comes from Vertex, what must
match SAP, and what FICO activities you perform before filing.

Follow-up 1:
Filing deadline is tomorrow; Vertex return is ready but SAP tax payable is short by $12,400.
You cannot find the invoices. What is your hour-by-hour plan?

Follow-up 2:
After filing, Vertex issues a amended return increasing liability. Cash already moved.
How do you book the amendment, interest/penalty if any, and explain it to FP&A?

Q17 — Internal order / investment order and tax
================================================================================
Original:
Capex is collected on an investment internal order, then settled to an asset.
Vendor invoices may include tax or require use tax. How should FICO ensure the
final asset value and tax treatment are correct with Vertex in play?

Follow-up 1:
Settlement already ran; tax was wrongly included in APC. Depreciation posted for two periods.
How do you correct asset values, tax accounts, and prior depreciation?

Follow-up 2:
Tax authority challenges capitalization of certain soft costs that were taxed differently
than hard costs. How do you re-class between asset, expense, and tax with full audit trail?

Q18 — Performance & high-volume billing with tax calls
================================================================================
Original:
Month-end billing of 200k lines is slow; teams blame Vertex calls. As FICO, how do you
separate true tax integration issues from SD pricing, FI posting, or batch job design?

Follow-up 1:
Business wants real-time tax on every simulation in VA01 and every invoice.
Infra says it will not scale. What compromise architecture and controls do you propose?

Follow-up 2:
A partial outage caused intermittent null tax on 0.3% of invoices that still posted.
How do you detect silent zero-tax postings and auto-block them going forward?

Q19 — Cross-border digital services (US nexus complexity)
================================================================================
Original:
The company sells SaaS subscriptions from a US company code to customers in many states.
There is no physical shipment. How do you think about tax determination with Vertex
and the FICO data you must get right (ship-to, sold-to, place of supply proxies)?

Follow-up 1:
Billing is monthly recurring via SD or Convergent Invoicing. Mid-cycle the customer
billing address changes. How should tax on credits and new charges behave?

Follow-up 2:
Product counsel reclassifies a SKU from SaaS to “data processing service” with different
taxability in five states. Build the business change plan across master data, open contracts,

Q20 — Stakeholder conflict: Tax vs Controllership vs IT
================================================================================
Original:
Tax wants maximum Vertex automation and minimal SAP tax codes. Controllership wants
simple GL and fast close. IT wants fewer custom exits. You are the SAP FICO lead.
How do you drive a decision in a design workshop?

Follow-up 1:
The workshop ends without agreement, and go-live is in six weeks. What interim operating
model do you put in place so the company can still invoice legally and close books?

Follow-up 2:
Post go-live, a severity-1 tax issue hits production. Tax blames SAP config, IT blames
Vertex content, Controllership blames master data. How do you run the war room and
what permanent RACI and monitoring do you install afterward?
